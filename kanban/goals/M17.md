# M17 — Lint epic

A user lints a file, edits it, and re-lints. The diagnostic delta between the two outputs should match the apparent scope of the edit. When it doesn't — when a small edit produces a disproportionate change in diagnostics, or a logically equivalent edit produces any change at all — that's a UX gap. M12 caught one flavor of this (whitespace-driven cascades: M12.16's brace-count gate, M12.17's component-close cascade, M12.18's HLSL classifier carve-out) and the lexinvariance harness in [`internal/harness/lexinvariance/`](../../internal/harness/lexinvariance/) is the substrate that surfaced them. [M13](M13.md) closed the specific case of inter-token-whitespace invariance as a hard property. More gaps have surfaced since; M17 sweeps the rest under one investigative front.

M17 is bounded by two principles:
- **B1.** Linting UX should be as pleasant as possible.
- **B2.** A linter can only do so much.

The work is staged: (1) a preliminary investigation that maps the edit→delta surprise space and produces a draft taxonomy; (2) a deeper investigation that root-causes each class and draws the B2 boundary (cascade bug vs. inherent linter limit); (3) a maximally complete fix within those bounds — fixing what can be fixed, documenting what can't. "Done" is re-pinned after phase 2; the goal commits to the shape of the work, not to a deliverable selected before the investigation has run.

**Scope:** the lint engine end-to-end (`internal/scan/`, `internal/parse/`, `internal/validate/`, `internal/lint/`, `internal/report/`), the lexinvariance and related harnesses in `internal/harness/`, and the WASM/playground surface (`worker/`, `cmd/voidslice-wasm/`) since the playground is the surface where edit→delta surprise actually bites users. Excludes: token-level substitution (different substrate, parked as a future goal); cross-file reference resolution (G-B); intra-token whitespace (part of the token, not a gap, and not a user-visible edit class). LSP-side rendering of diagnostic deltas stays with [M3](M3.md).
