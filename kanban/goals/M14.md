# M14 — Declared-typing spike

The schema-flavored work deferred from M4 was framed as two candidate goals (G-A: schema, G-B: cross-file refs); those labels stopped earning their keep. The conversation that produced this goal pressure-tested both intuitions against [`testdata/golden/`](../../testdata/golden/) and surfaced two facts that reframe the problem. First, the `.decl.xml` sibling files (12 in the golden corpus) are one-line `<ResourceInfo name="..." classname="..." isCompressed="..."/>` records — *declared* canonical logical name and *declared* kind per asset, eliminating the synthesis trap that killed M4's G-A; CLAUDE.md currently calls them "skipped by extension" (true at the linter level) and we've never mined them. Second, "self-declared inherit is high-confidence" cracks under inspection: suffix conventions are inconsistent across kinds and even within sibling entitydefs (`elite..def.decl` inherits `"...guard"`, `delilah..def.decl` inherits `"...witch.def"`), 100% of `inheritedDecl` references are unresolvable in our workspace, the `..<segment>.decl` filename grammar is undocumented, and engine semantics around missing inherits are unknown. Only one rule survived as immediately CI-shippable — `inheritedDecl` type-suffix consistency with the enclosing component, a pure intra-file check needing no resolution. M14 is a spike modeled on [M12-cascade-investigation.md](M12-cascade-investigation.md): convert these intuitions into measured facts plus minimal-repro fixtures, then propose the next concrete goal from a position of evidence rather than committing to G-A or G-B without it. Success: a written investigation report with measured filename↔declared-name round-trip rate, measured inherit-resolution rate per kind with the suffix-tolerance list any future resolver would have to encode, a go/no-go on the type-suffix check (and the diagnostic shipped if go), the gameN bucket gap stated as an acquisition question, and a named next-goal proposal at the bottom.

**Scope:** mining `.decl.xml` `<ResourceInfo>` metadata under [`testdata/golden/`](../../testdata/golden/), measuring filename↔declared-name and inherit-graph resolution against it, evaluating the one surviving intra-file diagnostic candidate (`inheritedDecl` type-suffix consistency with the enclosing component) for shippability, and writing the findings into [`kanban/goals/M14.md`](M14.md) sibling files in the M12-cascade-investigation shape. The shippability bar is bounded by a partial-context constraint: the linter's primary surface is the web Playground, where users paste raw text *and* drag-and-drop files in the same session, so a given lint run sees N files with 0–N filenames and 0–M sibling `.decl.xml` records present independently — every rule whose evidence depends on filename or `<ResourceInfo>` (audits A and B) must therefore apply when the context is present, silently skip when it isn't, and never paper over absence with a guess (the same load-bearing rule M13 documented for whitespace-invariance: path-based skips can't apply to the surface that actually serves users). Audit C (the intra-file type-suffix check) is unaffected — it works on the parsed token stream alone — and its survival of this filter is part of why it's the only currently-shippable candidate. Committed minimal-repro fixtures only where they sharpen a finding (e.g., the suffix-convention split between `elite..def.decl` and `delilah..def.decl`). Excludes building an `internal/schema/` package; building a file-graph data structure beyond what the audits strictly need; shipping any diagnostic except the type-suffix check (and only if its FP rate is empirically near-zero **and** it degrades gracefully under the partial-context constraint); answering the gameN bucket question (no fixtures for it yet — the spike states the gap and what acquisition would be required). Reuses the Handler interface in [`internal/parse/parse.go`](../../internal/parse/parse.go) (`OnComponentDecl`, `OnAssignment`) and the corpus-walker pattern from [`internal/lint/`](../../internal/lint/) `TestCleanSweep` / `TestCoverageAudit`. Adjacent to [M4](M4.md), whose deferred G-A/G-B candidate-goal framings this spike retires in favor of a measured proposal.
