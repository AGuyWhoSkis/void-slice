# M12 — Lint engine hardening

The linter's golden-TDD harness checks soundness on known-good files: zero diagnostics on a corpus of valid game data. It is silent on soundness *under mutation* — the property that a localized corruption produces only localized diagnostics. M11's investigation surfaced a canonical violation: an unterminated quote produces diagnostics on downstream lines that were valid in the unmutated file and re-validate clean once the quote is closed. The axiom this goal anchors to is *a diagnostic should never be wrong*; a diagnostic that only appears because of upstream corruption is exactly that — wrong, and undetectable by the existing harness. M12 closes the gap by building a property-based locality harness over mutated goldens (per the taxonomy and recommendation in [linter-hardening-prospects.md](linter-hardening-prospects.md)) and fixing whatever cascades the investigation surfaces. Success: the harness lands and runs alongside the existing test/harness suite, the cascade repro fixture is committed, and the cascade shapes characterized by the investigation are either fixed or filed forward with explicit rationale.

**Scope:** the soundness harness itself (property-based mutation of golden files plus a locality-property assertion) and any scanner/parser/validator fixes the investigation surfaces. The first ticket is investigation-only — commit the cascade repro under [`testdata/`](../../testdata/), characterize cascade shapes across the corpus (unterminated quote, brace, block comment, …), and stress-test the technique pick against the evidence, diverging only if findings demand it. Excludes inferring author intent; completeness oracles (idParser differential, Void Engine round-trips, missing-diagnostic hunting — a future goal); and cross-corpus differential between D2 / DOTO / Deathloop unless investigation surfaces cross-corpus cascades. Adjacent to [M4](M4.md), whose clean-sweep harness anchors the soundness-on-known-good half of this work, and to [M11](M11.md), whose memo handed off the M12 framing in [linter-hardening-prospects.md](linter-hardening-prospects.md).
