# M4.1.3 — Lint walkers: include flat-root corpus files in clean-sweep / coverage-audit

**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M4
**Size:** small
**Origin:** M4.1.2

## What

`TestCleanSweep` ([internal/lint/clean_sweep_test.go](../../internal/lint/clean_sweep_test.go)) and `TestCoverageAudit` ([internal/lint/coverage_audit_test.go](../../internal/lint/coverage_audit_test.go)) walk only `corpusRoot/d2/game1` and `corpusRoot/doto/game1`. CLAUDE.md's Corpus section and several ticket-writing assumptions (including M4.1.2's "feed clean-sweep / coverage-audit walkers" wording) treat the flat root of `testdata/golden/` as part of the swept corpus. It isn't. The ~50+ flat-root fixtures (`generated.decls.*.decl`, `*.decl.xml`, plus the new `eof.*.decl` fixtures from M4.1.2) are dormant — present on disk but not actually linted by either test. Discovered while reviewing where M4.1.2's new EOF fixtures would be exercised.

## Scope

- Broaden `TestCleanSweep` and `TestCoverageAudit` to walk `corpusRoot` directly (recursive `filepath.WalkDir` already covers subdirs), or add a third `walkDir(corpusRoot)` call in addition to the existing two. Pick whichever produces no double-walk of `d2/game1` / `doto/game1`.
- Decide what to do with `*.decl.xml` (XML, not the `.decl` grammar). Either filter on `filepath.Ext == ".decl.xml"` and skip, or let them flow through the existing `parseGapFiles` allowlist if they emit only `PARSE_UNEXPECTED_TOKEN` / `VOID_SCAN_STRUCTURE` / etc. The clean-sweep allowlist (`fileHasParseGap`) was built precisely for this kind of corpus-ahead-of-grammar gap.
- Update CLAUDE.md's Corpus section to accurately describe what the walkers walk. The current text says flat-root files are "walked by" these tests — make that true (preferred) or remove the claim.

## Dependencies

None. (M4.1.2 is closed; its `eof.*.decl` fixtures are the canary that this ticket validates against.)

## Verification

- `TestCleanSweep` and `TestCoverageAudit` walk the flat root without `t.Errorf` regressions. Any new diagnostics from flat-root files are either expected (parse-gap allowlisted) or filtered by extension.
- M4.1.2's `eof.block-comment-unterminated.decl` shows up in `TestCoverageAudit`'s `codeCount["VOID_SCAN"]` tally (`t.Logf` output).
- `*.decl.xml` files either skip cleanly or produce only allowlisted parse-gap diagnostics — no `t.Errorf`.
- CLAUDE.md's Corpus section matches reality after the change.
- `go test ./...` and `go vet ./...` clean.

## Notes

- This is the documentation/coverage half of the M4.1 audit chain. M4.1.1 audited `VOID_SCAN`-emitting branches, M4.1.2 fixed the two EOF defects + added fixtures, M4.1.3 makes those fixtures actually visible to the coverage walkers.
- Recommend broadening over documenting-down: the gap-tracking infra (`fileHasParseGap`, `parseGapFiles`) was built for this. Documenting-down would lose real coverage on the flat-root corpus that was committed precisely to be exercised.
