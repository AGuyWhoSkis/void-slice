# M12.5 · Fix unterminated-brace diagnostic locality

**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M12
**Size:** small
**Origin:** M12.1

## What

Fix the parser-layer locality violation where deleting a closing `}` produces `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` at the position of the *last token before EOF*, not at the position of the missing brace. §2 of [`kanban/goals/M12-cascade-investigation.md`](../goals/M12-cascade-investigation.md) found this consistently across both formats and both games: the diagnostic lands 1 line earlier than the deleted brace, range `[1..1]` for all 17 samples (6 spot-checks + 11 corpus).

Smaller scope than M12.3/M12.4 — no scanner-layer recovery, no synthetic tokens, just a column/line fix in where the parser anchors the diagnostic. But it's a real locality violation (`a diagnostic should never be wrong` includes "the location should not be wrong") and the M12.2 harness will catch it at line-window W=0.

Repro fixture: [`testdata/cascades/unterminated-brace/minimal.decl`](../../testdata/cascades/unterminated-brace/minimal.decl). Current behavior emits `PARSE_UNTERMINATED_OBJECT` at line 2 (the opening `{`, correct) plus `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` × 2 at line 3 col 9 (end of the last token, *not* the missing-brace's natural position).

## Scope

- **Layer:** parser ([`internal/parse/`](../../internal/parse/)).
- **The fix:** when the parser reaches EOF inside an unclosed object, anchor the `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` at the EOF position (or at the position of the matching opening `{`, paired with `PARSE_UNTERMINATED_OBJECT`), not at the column-of-last-token. Two reasonable anchorings:
  - At EOF (the position the missing `}` would have occupied).
  - At the opening `{` (where the unterminated-object diagnostic already points). This effectively folds the two diagnostics together.
- **Pick at implementation:** check whether the duplicate `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` (currently emitted twice — "expected '}' to close object value" + "expected '}' to close top-level object") is intended or accidental. If accidental, dedupe.
- **Tests:** parser-level tests under `internal/parse/`. The change shifts the line/col of existing diagnostics on a small set of test fixtures; update assertions accordingly.

## Dependencies

None (M12.1 closed). M12.2 (harness) is a sibling.
