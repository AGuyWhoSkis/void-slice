# M12.6 · Tighten EOF anchor so locality harness passes corpus-wide at W=0

**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M12
**Size:** small
**Origin:** M12.5

## What

The M12.5 fix anchors EOF-cascade parser diagnostics at `len(src)` so a
missing close-brace lands on the line the user would edit. That's correct on
two of the three trailing-byte shapes:

| Original tail | Mutated tail (after `}` delete) | Anchor line via `len(src)` | mutLine | Match? |
|---------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------|---------|--------|
| `...content}` (no trailing `\n`) | `...content` | last content line | last content line | ✓ |
| `...content\n}` (committed fixture) | `...content\n` | empty line past content | empty line past content | ✓ |
| `...content\n}\n` (trailing `\n`) | `...content\n\n` | empty line past *both* newlines | line `}` was on | ✗ (+1 off) |

The third shape is common in the corpus (most goldens end with `}\n`), so the
M12.2 locality harness runs at W=1 to absorb the +1 anchor. M12.5's
verification contract specified W=0 — currently met only by
`TestLocalityCommittedFixturesAtW0` (the three committed cascade fixtures)
plus the bare-`}` shape, not the `}\n`-tail shape across the corpus.

After this fix, `TestLocalityHarness` runs at W=0 and the corpus-wide
trailing-`\n` shape lands on the deleted `}`'s line.

Repro: revert `localityWindow` in
[`internal/harness/locality/locality_test.go`](../../internal/harness/locality/locality_test.go)
from `1` to `0`. `go test ./internal/harness/locality/...` produces ~12
locality violations of the form `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL at N:c (span lines N..N)`
where `mutLine = N+1`. Every violation is the same +1 EOF-anchor shape.

## Scope

- **Layer:** parser ([`internal/parse/parse.go`](../../internal/parse/parse.go)) — `eofSpan` only.
- **The fix:** detect when the source has two or more trailing newlines and
  anchor at the position of the first of them, so PosAt maps to the line the
  user would edit (between the trailing newlines rather than past all of
  them). Handle CRLF (`\r\n\r\n`) symmetrically — anchor at the offset of
  the first `\n` in the trailing pair, *not* the `\r`.
- **Harness:** flip `localityWindow` from 1 to 0 in
  `locality_test.go` and rewrite its docstring to match. The
  `TestLocalityCommittedFixturesAtW0` test becomes redundant once the broad
  sweep covers W=0; collapse it.
- **Tests:** the existing parse and report goldens use no-trailing-newline
  fixtures, so they shouldn't shift. Re-run with `-update` if any do.

## Dependencies

M12.5 (closed). No other prereqs.

## Verification

- `TestLocalityHarness` passes with `localityWindow = 0`.
- `go test ./...` and `go vet ./...` clean.
- `make harnesses` green.
- Spot-check: linting a `}\n`-tail corpus file with the last `}` deleted
  produces `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` on the line the `}` was originally on,
  not the line past it.

## Out of scope

- Reworking the parser's `diagSpan` for non-EOF callers — they're already
  correct.
- Cross-corpus differential — still deferred per [M12.md](../goals/M12.md).
