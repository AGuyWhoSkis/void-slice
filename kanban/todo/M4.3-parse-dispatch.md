## What

Implement the routing layer described in [M4-decl-taxonomy.md § Dispatch decision](../goals/M4-decl-taxonomy.md): extension picks XML vs text; content-sniff picks among shapes 1–5. This is the **parser-layer entry point** ticket; M4.4–M4.7 fill in the per-shape grammars behind it.
