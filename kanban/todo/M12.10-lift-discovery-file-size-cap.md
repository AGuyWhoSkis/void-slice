# M12.10 · Lift the 32 KB discovery file-size cap

**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M12
**Size:** medium
**Origin:** M12.9

## What

The M12.9 discovery driver caps the corpus sweep at files ≤ 32 KB
(`maxScanBytes` in
[`internal/harness/discovery/discovery_test.go`](../../internal/harness/discovery/discovery_test.go)).
The cap was forced by a per-file O(F²) cost (`mutations ∝ F`, `lint ∝ F`)
that turns the spot-checked 7.7 MB `d2/game1/.../dunwall_escape_tower_p.entities`
into ~70 hours of work.

17 of the 67 corpus files currently skip — including every file the
[M12 cascade memo](../goals/M12-cascade-investigation.md) singles out as
the highest-signal targets. Whatever novel cascade lives in the big
`.entities` files is exactly what discovery should be hunting; the cap
silently excludes that surface.

## Scope

- **Profile** the inner loop on a representative ~250 KB file (the
  excerpt `.entities`, already in the corpus): split time between
  mutation enumeration, `applyMutation`, `lint.New().Lint`, and the
  signal-evaluation pass. Identify whether the bottleneck is parser
  work, allocator churn from per-mutation byte copies, or the validator.
- **Choose a strategy** based on the profile. Two non-mutually-exclusive
  candidates:
  - *Per-file mutation budget.* For files above some threshold, sample
    K mutations (deterministic — e.g. stride-`N`) rather than
    enumerate. Add `Options.MaxMutations` to discovery.Scan with 0 = no
    cap; the driver picks the budget. Strict M12.9 "exhaustive" only
    holds below the threshold.
  - *Hot-path optimization.* If `lint` itself is the bottleneck,
    consider sharing `scan.Scan` output across mutations whose
    structural-mask doesn't overlap the diff window (likely complex —
    revisit only if profiling shows >50% of time in the scanner).
- **Re-enable** the 17 skipped files at the chosen budget. Confirm the
  driver finishes in under ~10 min on the full corpus and the report
  reflects the wider scan.
- **Document** the budget rationale in
  [`internal/harness/discovery/discovery_test.go`](../../internal/harness/discovery/discovery_test.go)
  alongside the (raised or removed) cap.

## Dependencies

M12.9 (closed — the harness must exist before we can tune its budget).

## Verification

- `go test -tags=discovery ./internal/harness/discovery/` runs to
  completion on the full corpus in under 10 min on a dev box.
- The generated `discovery-report.md` no longer lists the 17 skipped
  files in its `Skipped` section (or lists fewer with a justified
  threshold).
- `go test ./...` and `go vet ./...` clean. `make harnesses` unaffected.

## Out of scope

- New mutation kinds. Stay within the existing structural-byte set.
- Multi-byte / token-level mutations. M12.9 deliberately scoped to
  single-byte; revisit only after the corpus-wide signal is understood.
