# M4.1.1 — `VOID_SCAN` audit on the `.decl` corpus (incl. CRLF)

**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M4
**Origin:** M4.1

The M4.1 inventory found **2,786 `VOID_SCAN` diagnostics** across the golden corpus, ~98% concentrated in scope-relevant shapes:

- Shape 4 (renderprog): 2,388 — out of scope per G-C, but counts here for completeness
- Shape 3 (material): 341
- Sidecar `.decl.xml`: 36
- Shape 2 (animset): 16
- Shape 1 (curly inherit/edit): 5

`VOID_SCAN` is a scanner diagnostic, not a parser one — these fire before the parser ever sees the tokens, which means they will *not* drain when the M4 grammar tickets land. They need their own pass.

A separate observation from M4.1: the entire `testdata/corpus-mini/intake/` corpus uses **CRLF line endings**. Worth confirming the scanner handles CRLF cleanly, since some non-trivial slice of the `VOID_SCAN` count may be CRLF-related rather than genuine lex defects.

## Inputs

- Per-shape `VOID_SCAN` counts and the 70-file corpus inventory: [kanban/goals/M4-decl-taxonomy.md](../goals/M4-decl-taxonomy.md).
- Scanner: [internal/scan/](../../internal/scan/) (esp. `scan.go`, `scan_util.go`).

## Deliverables

1. **Branch-by-branch audit.** For each scanner branch that emits `VOID_SCAN`, count its hits on the corpus and classify: real defect (fix), known limitation (allowlist with rationale), or noise (silence the branch). Recommend reading the diagnostic Span context for a sample of each branch's hits before deciding.
2. **CRLF handling check.** Confirm the scanner treats `\r\n` line endings as equivalent to `\n` — for newline-offset tracking, comment-line termination, and any `\r`-as-whitespace assumptions. If there's a gap, fix it; CRLF is the dominant style in the intake corpus and will be the dominant style in any real-world Dishonored 2 / DOTO file the user lints.
3. **Code changes** (if any) in `internal/scan/`. Update or add unit tests covering the fixes.
4. **Allowlist update.** Adjust the M4.2 starting allowlist (per [M4-decl-taxonomy.md](../goals/M4-decl-taxonomy.md)#allowlist-inventory-for-m42) to reflect the post-audit counts. Shape 4's residual `VOID_SCAN` stays allowlisted (G-C); everything else should drain or be allowlisted with a reason.

## Done when

- The 2,786 `VOID_SCAN` count drops to a defensible residual (Shape 4 only, or Shape 4 + a small documented set of allowlisted hits).
- CRLF is confirmed handled cleanly, with a regression test.
- M4.2's allowlist inventory in [M4-decl-taxonomy.md](../goals/M4-decl-taxonomy.md) is updated with the new counts.

## Notes

- Strictly a scanner-layer ticket. No grammar work — that's the M4.2-onward stream.
- M4.2 (golden corpus regression harness) does not block on this; it can ship with the un-drained `VOID_SCAN` allowlist and tighten as M4.1.1 lands. If they're worked in parallel, the `VOID_SCAN` allowlist line just changes once.
