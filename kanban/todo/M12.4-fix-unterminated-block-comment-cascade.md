# M12.4 · Fix unterminated-block-comment cascade

**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M12
**Size:** small
**Origin:** M12.1

## What

Fix the scanner-layer cascade where `/*` without a matching `*/` consumes everything to EOF as one comment, deceiving the parser about every closing brace and statement-terminator that fell inside the over-extended comment. §2 of [`kanban/goals/M12-cascade-investigation.md`](../goals/M12-cascade-investigation.md) confirms: on `.decl` files (D2 and DOTO), the cascade is consistently +1 `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` diagnostic at EOF; on `.entities` files the parser hasn't yet entered a block when the comment swallows the rest of the file, so no cascade emerges.

Smaller blast radius than M12.3 (one extra diagnostic, not thousands), but the same family of fix: scanner-layer recovery for an unterminated-X construct that's swallowing structural tokens. The two fixes may share a recovery design; M12.3's implementer should flag any shared abstraction before M12.4 starts.

Repro fixture: [`testdata/cascades/unterminated-block-comment/minimal.decl`](../../testdata/cascades/unterminated-block-comment/minimal.decl). After the fix, linting it should emit exactly 1 `VOID_SCAN unterminated block comment` diagnostic on line 2 — no `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` cascade.

## Scope

- **Layer:** scanner ([`internal/scan/`](../../internal/scan/)).
- **Recovery strategy:** the scanner already emits `VOID_SCAN unterminated block comment`. The fix is to **not** continue treating the bytes from `/*` to EOF as one comment for the purpose of feeding the parser — terminate the comment at EOF, but don't let it swallow the closing `}` etc. that the parser expects. Concretely, the parser's `PARSE_EXPECTED_SYMBOL` cascade comes from the parser asserting at EOF that the `{` from line 1 should have a matching `}` — which is correct *information*, but the cascade-y feel comes from it being one line up from the actual fault (the unterminated comment). After the fix, the parser should either (a) suppress that follow-up assertion when a scanner-layer fault has already been emitted, or (b) the scanner should emit a synthetic `}` to balance the open `{` and let the parser unwind clean.
- **Design question (resolve at implementation time):** is suppression in the parser the right place, or should the scanner emit a synthetic close-brace? M12.3 will likely answer this — the unterminated-quote fix faces the same "scanner ate the closer" problem, just with quotes instead of braces. Pick the same approach for both unless evidence demands otherwise.
- **Tests:** scanner goldens update where the fix touches them; parser tests may need a "scanner-fault-already-emitted, suppress follow-up" expectation.
