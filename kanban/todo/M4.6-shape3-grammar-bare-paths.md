**Status:** todo
**Goal:** M4
**Size:** medium

## What

Shape 3 is the material grammar: 3 corpus files, tab-aligned key-value pairs, no `=`, no `;`, with bare-path values (`models/.../arm.tga`) and namespaced option keys (`wrinkles/enable`, `hasBumpMap`). It contributes 502 `PARSE_UNEXPECTED_TOKEN` and **244 of the 828 residual `VOID_SCAN`** — the latter from the scanner's catch-all "unknown byte" branch on `/` (211) and `.` (33). Per the taxonomy doc, those drain "when the Shape 3 grammar ticket teaches the scanner or grammar to recognize bare paths." This ticket lands the Shape-3 walker and the scanner support together because they're the same logical change.

This is the **scanner-layer + parser-layer Shape-3 golden coverage** ticket.

## Scope

- Scanner change to recognize `/` and `.` in bare-path / namespaced-key positions without emitting `VOID_SCAN`. Implementation detail (grammar-driven vs. token-class promotion) decided at PLAN time; constraint is that existing `goldenFileNames` byte-pinned spot-checks keep passing or are intentionally re-baselined with justification.
- New parser walker for Shape 3: tab-aligned key-value pairs, top-level `m_PhysicsMaterial "…"`, `version N`, nested `state { }` and `options { … }` blocks.
- Walker fronts Shape 3 dispatch route (`m_PhysicsMaterial` content-sniff per M4.3).
